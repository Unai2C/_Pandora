# Design seeds

2026-09-18 · Owner · A collaborative Decentraland game inspired by Pandora from Avatar. Players travel between islands, find treasures, gather resources and defeat enemies. Their actions expand a village with buildings, constructions and properties. Characters also progress through RPG levels and abilities.

2026-09-18 · Existing visual reference · `../concept_Pandora.png` shows floating islands, luminous vegetation, bridges, settlements and a central crystal. Its platform and player-count labels are concept-art text, not verified requirements or performance evidence.

2026-09-18 · Pending interview · Village ownership, contribution rules, expedition structure, combat, skills and the meaning of properties have not been decided. Public title and how the Avatar inspiration becomes the game's own setting remain undecided.

2026-09-18 · Owner · Three expedition activities feed village development: gathering resources, hunting or defeating enemies, and finding treasures. Deliveries include food, treasures that increase wealth, and construction materials such as iron and wood.
2026-09-18 · Reference location · The concept image remains at C:/Users/Imagine To Create/Desktop/dcl_Pandora/concept_Pandora.png; the earlier relative image reference is superseded by this absolute path.
2026-09-20 · Owner · Possible teleportation to selected points depending on character skills · Tentative addition to physical travel; not yet selected for implementation.
2026-09-20 · Agent · Initial skill roster proposal: explorer gathering yield and treasure detection; warrior attack damage and durability; mage projectile and ally healing; basic attack available to all · Not yet confirmed ability by ability.
2026-09-20 · Owner · Proposed class paths: Ranger→Hunter→Sniper; Ranger→Tracker→Trapper; Ranger→Tamer→Beastmaster; Wizard→Cleric→White Mage; Wizard→Sorcerer→Pyromancer; Wizard→Illusionist→Summoner; Warrior→Arquer→Crusader; Warrior→Barbarian→Berserker; Warrior→Knight→Commander. Requests a different top rank for Arquer. Concrete abilities and Arcane Archer alternative drafted in class-paths-proposal.md; not yet adopted.
