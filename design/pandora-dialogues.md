## Tutorial

### 1. GATHER FRUIT

I am Pandora. We agents come from Novaterra, an ancient city now in ruins. Together we can rebuild it. First, gather fruit: the settlement needs food. Click a marked bush.

### 2. GATHER WOOD

Food is the first step toward a living community. Take this axe. Gather wood to rebuild Novaterra’s shelters. Select 2 and press E near a tree, or click it.

### 3. MINE STONE

Wood gives us shelter; stone will give us lasting foundations. Take this pickaxe and mine stone to restore our city. Select 3 and press E near a rock, or click it.

### 4. RECOVER MEMORY SHARDS

Some agents have lost control. Recover their memory shards to restore Novaterra’s past. Take this sword: select 1 and press E near an agent outside the village. If you are hurt, return to the central tree’s aura. It heals you quickly whatever your maximum health. Wait for HEALTH FULL before heading out again.

### 5. TUTORIAL COMPLETE

Every resource helps rebuild Novaterra. The shards recovered from agents that have lost control let us piece the village’s memory back together. Bring what you have gathered to any of the four delivery altars at the corners of the central platform. Their resources are shared by the whole village.
