## Level semantics

Start at level 1 with the great bioluminescent tree at the center and the resource-delivery point around its base. The level-1 hub also provides physical access points for the shop and skill board. Each row is the additional cost to complete that current level, not a cumulative total. Its listed village state is available on entering the level. Completing levels 1–12 enters the next level and applies its building state. Completing level 13 records a completed cycle, then starts level 1. The last row's completion event happens only after meeting its requirements. Services open through one interaction with their physical building or station and reuse the inherited UI windows and layout.

Initial phase: levels 1–4. Intermediate: 5–8. Advanced: 9–13. These bands affect village requirements, not permission to explore islands or learn personal skills. Building changes use the already proposed seven structures; other rows are visual upgrades, not extra systems.

## Cycle reset — scope accepted

[Owner decision · 2026-10-04] Completing a cycle grants each eligible participant 100 town coins and a cycle-exclusive badge. Eligibility requires at least one qualifying contribution during that cycle: delivering resources or supplies, or helping defeat an enemy under the existing combat participation rules. Visiting alone does not qualify. Every eligible player receives the same guaranteed reward; do not rank contributors or scale rewards by contribution volume. The 100-coin amount is an approved initial balance value, not playtested. If an eligible player is offline when the cycle completes, deliver the reward automatically at their next login.

[Owner decision · 2026-10-04] Players may join an ongoing village cycle at any time. They can contribute immediately and qualify for that cycle's reward by meeting the same participation rule; there is no cycle-entry lock or waiting period.

## Personal participation badges

Owner decision, 2026-09-20: a character receives one personal badge for each village cycle in which they participated. Badges survive village resets, grant no combat advantage and remain in the owner's private profile. [agent-decided · accepted] Award one badge on cycle completion to eligible resource/supply deliverers and qualifying combat helpers, including offline contributors; merely visiting does not qualify. All eligible contributors also receive 100 town coins, delivered at next login when offline. Do not display cycle counts beside player names or imply an external token/wearable.




## World-phase mapping

[agent-decided · accepted] Village levels 1–4 correspond to the initial phase, levels 5–8 to the intermediate phase and levels 9–13 to the advanced phase. These bands communicate difficulty and village development; they do not lock island access. Every island is physically explorable from the beginning, although advanced enemies are much more dangerous to new characters. Village buildings and their services appear automatically only when their assigned village level is reached.
